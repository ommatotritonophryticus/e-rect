E-Rect for PSP
==============

Install
-------
Copy the PSP folder from this archive to the root of your Memory Stick, so you
end up with:

    ms0:/PSP/GAME/ERECT/EBOOT.PBP
    ms0:/PSP/GAME/ERECT/pack/pack1/*.wav
    ms0:/PSP/GAME/ERECT/pack/pack2/*.wav

Then start it from the PSP's Game menu (Memory Stick).

Two soundtracks ship and one is drawn at random every time you reach the menu
and every time you start a run, so a session moves between them. Both have to be
present: they are held in memory together, which is what makes the change
instant instead of a pause while six megabytes are re-read.

If the game cannot find them the title screen says so along the bottom, so if it
is quiet, look there first. In an emulator the path is relative to whatever you
have configured as the memory stick root, not to wherever the EBOOT itself was
opened from.

Homebrew needs custom firmware. On stock firmware the EBOOT will not launch.
Tested in PPSSPP 1.20.4; not verified on real hardware.

Controls
--------
    D-pad / analog nub   move
    Cross                jump
    Square               attack
    Circle / Down        ground slam - puts up the wall
    Start                pause menu
    Home                 quit

The field runs on forever left and right; the view follows you. Enemies come to
wherever you are, so running does not lose them for long.

Wounded players bank energy for the wall faster - up to three times as fast on
the brink.

Waves
-----
Every fifth wave is a boss wave. Wave 10 belongs to a single flying boss: twice
the size of an ordinary flyer, and it blinks away every time you land a hit.

A wave can carry up to two modifiers, one of each kind, announced during the
countdown:

    who turns up      GROUND ONLY, FLYERS ONLY, HORDE, JUMPERS
    how it plays      HOLD THE LINE  view is pinned, you start on full health
                      GROUNDED       no jumping; down raises the wall, longer reach
                      NO WALL        the slam buys invulnerability; landing heals
                      BLIND          you cannot see them, they can barely hurt you

Nothing explodes for damage. Red blinkers leave a blast and reappear at the edge
of the field when touched, and once more the first time you hit one. Shooters
show a white sight that turns red for the last 100 ms before they fire - that is
the moment to step out of the line.

Music and effect volumes are in Settings.
Settings and high scores are written to ms0:/PSP/GAME/ERECT/settings.dat
