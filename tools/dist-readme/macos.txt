E-Rect for macOS (Apple Silicon)
================================

Run ./erect. Keep the packs folder next to it - that is where the music and
sound effects live. Without it the game still runs, just silently.

Gatekeeper
----------
This binary is not signed or notarised, so the first launch is refused. Either
right-click it in Finder and choose Open, which offers a one-time exception, or
strip the quarantine flag from a terminal:

    xattr -d com.apple.quarantine erect

Requirements
------------
Apple Silicon, macOS 11 or newer. Nothing else to install: audio goes through
CoreAudio, gamepads through IOKit, both part of the system.

Controls
--------
    Player 1   W A S D, SPACE to attack
    Player 2   arrow keys, ENTER to attack
    Gamepads   supported; pick a scheme per player in Settings
    ESC        pause

    move left/right   walk          jump   leave the ground
    down while airborne             ground slam (puts up the wall)

Settings and high scores go to
~/Library/Application Support/erect/settings.json
